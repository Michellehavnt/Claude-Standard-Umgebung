# AffiliateFinder ICP Research — Verbatim Insights from AFbrain Sales Calls

*Compiled 2026-07-22. Source: 525 analyzed AFbrain sales calls → 205 ideal-fit (business email, gmail excluded) → 28 top-scored calls deep-read in full. Speakers attributed; quotes verbatim.*

## Who actually books calls (observed segments)

1. **Brand-side affiliate/marketing leads** — Head of Affiliate Program (CloudTalk), Head of Affiliate Marketing (Volza), Head of Marketing (Uniqode, Aspire Partners), Managing Director (Waters Co), Founders (Wine Ambassador, Accounting Doctor). Programs exist but are stagnant, neglected, or full of low-quality partners.
2. **Affiliate/partnership agencies** — Searlco, Havas, Loads of Traffic, Aumento, Jayger (customer), 1Veritas, 100.partners, Traffic Park, Fishbat, Astutr, Adfuel, Upcode. Recruit publishers for client brands; care about volume limits, multi-brand pricing, white-label.
3. **Network-side** — Awin VP ("I think this tool would actually do a better job than we're currently doing"), Tradedoubler ("tools like you are completely invaluable" for their 2,000+ self-serve clients).
4. **Program newbies** — brands that just opened an Awin/CJ account and have done "nothing, nothing" (Digiteau), or "don't even know what the industry standards are" (Colab Army).

## A. Top pains (ranked by frequency/intensity)

1. **Finding *qualified* affiliates is the bottleneck** — "my biggest pain point… is just getting qualified affiliates" (David Fertig, roseperl.com); "The hardest part is just finding the good affiliate" (Chris Sammarone, upcode.com); "the most effort that you have to put into the program is to acquire the right publishers… [brands] just count on the luck" (Dimitri Reichert, trafficpark.net).
2. **Discovery tools dump lists without next steps** — "it will bring you a huge list that is perhaps not perfectly relevant. There's difficulties in getting the contacts… you're kind of just left with this kind of like, what do I do now?… There's a missing connect in there" (David James, VP, Awin).
3. **Junk volume beats signal** — "There's no point having 10,000 results when 9,900 is s***. You may as well just concentrate on the hundred" (Andy Reeve, CEO, Searlco).
4. **Unvetted affiliates / fake signups** — "they're just not even real people sometimes… they signed up and then disappear" (Fertig); "affiliates… might sign up and never post" (Sammarone); "the person that ran it was very lax on who he let into our partner program" (Summer Otey, Uniqode).
5. **Manual prospecting doesn't scale** — "we'd get our database together, find all the contacts, put them into a sheet and reach out manually" (Jack Perks, loadsoftraffic.com); "five Koreans all sitting next to each other… each one searching a hashtag and manually DMing on Instagram" (Astutr, re a client); "I've been doing it manually and it takes a lot of time" (Hana Strečanská, Head of Affiliate Program, CloudTalk).
6. **Contact data gap** — "Contacts and stuff is very, very difficult now" (Reeve); info@ vs owner emails ("How do I add James-at… instead of the info-at?" — Jani Gysels, Jayger); "we got no responses from the emails, I don't know if these emails are verified" (Daniel Pasco, adfuel.me).
7. **Outreach gets ignored / inbox fatigue on both sides** — "we got very less responses" (Rakesh Kumar, Volza); "any decent [affiliate is] getting bombarded… I just press the Delete button on 99.9%" (Reeve).
8. **Stagnant programs & channel dependence** — "it's kind of been stagnant ish for the last couple years… living in the background" (Summer, Uniqode); "very, very heavily reliant on Meta and Google and we want to switch things up" (Scott Muir, Waters Co).
9. **Incumbent tools: expensive, limited, cumbersome** — Publisher Discovery "a bit cumbersome and… labor intensive" (Awin VP), churned by Searlco over "limitations"; Grin "$24k a year" (Astutr); Connecto/Affiliate AI "commercials… quite significant" (Havas); Winmo login limits; "I have 50 tools like this already" (Fertig).

## B. Goals (their words)

- "Expand our affiliate network… some good kind of affiliate who can convert, who can drive quality traffic" (Rakesh, Volza)
- "We're sitting around 10, 15k a quarter [affiliate revenue]. We want to grow that a lot" + "go after people that are invested in [our niche]" (Summer, Uniqode)
- "It's the discovery more than the email… finding the right engagement" (Reeve, Searlco)
- "Going out and finding new partners… putting them into the partner pipeline, and then even starting to reach out" (Tyler Gillespie, 100.partners)
- "If there's a way of making it quicker, faster, smoother, easier, we're all for it" (Nick Hubble, loadsoftraffic)
- "Find lookalikes — similar partners to the existing ones which perform well" (Hana, CloudTalk)
- Agencies: roll out "across every single brand that we've got" (Hamish, Havas)

## C. Exact vocabulary for copy

**Their nouns:** affiliate program / partner program, publishers, partners, qualified affiliates, quality traffic, outreach / outbound outreach, recruitment, discovery, verified emails, contact details, partner pipeline, ideal partner profile, lookalikes, verticals, geos, commercials (=pricing), workflows.
**Their verbs:** grow / expand / scale the program, recruit publishers, acquire the right publishers, reach out to the right people, identify, vet, onboard, monetize.
**Their color:** "hunting tool", "count on the luck", "held together with duct tape and super glue", "sign up and never post", "throwing crap at the wall emails", "bombarded", "what do I do now?", "duct tape", "heaven send", "Rocket Reach for influencers".

## D. Status quo to attack in copy

Networks' own directories + PartnerStack invites; spreadsheets + manual email; Hunter/Apollo/ListKit point-tool stacks ("Appify to Apollo to Exa AI… it's not as good" — Gillespie); trade-show lists; hashtag search + manual DMs; or nothing at all ("we're not actually actively seeking affiliates" — Waters Co).

## E. Competitor mentions

Publisher Discovery (churn: cumbersome, limits; enterprise ≈ $500/mo unlimited = Searlco's anchor), Grin ($24k/yr), Connecto, Affiliate AI, Modash, Winmo, Similarweb/Semrush, Apollo, Hunter, ListKit, Smartlead, Lemlist, Instantly, PartnerStack AI agent ("are we actually getting the right people?" — Uniqode).

## F. Emotional drivers, objections, pricing psychology

- **Price delight at $99–125/mo**: "Oh, wow. I'm very interested… I'm honestly ready to go" (Paul Vassau); "You're not charging hundreds of dollars a month, so this makes it possible" (Sammarone); "my CEO will be more comfortable doing this monthly… I will get an approval for 99" (Ammar).
- **Time-saved framing**: "for 100 bucks a month… just for the amount of time it'll save me" (Fertig); "We're always looking for tools that can save us time" (Awin).
- **Trial de-risks**: "I end up signing up for 7 free days and I can always cancel" (Adam Remis); "Can I sign up for another seven day trial?" (Kurt Ohlson); "We always test it out on ourselves first before we throw clients into it" (Fishbat).
- **Proof demanded**: "Has any other CPA firms used your service before?"; "Is there any success with your clients?"; "Are you guys super new… in an MVP-like mode?"
- **Quality skepticism to preempt**: "How are they qualified as affiliates?" (Scott Darrohn, Fishbat); "are we basically just emailing them out of the blue?" (Brett, Wine Ambassador).
- **Ads work**: "you're smashing the social retargeting ads. I literally have you everywhere on my Instagram… I can't escape you" (Jodie Millward, Tradedoubler); "I was targeted clearly very, very heavily" (Havas).
- **Customer-love quotes (get permission before publishing!)**: "By the time I have written down something that frustrates me… the feature is there the next day"; "You guys are really user centric" (Jani Gysels, jayger.media).

## Copy implications applied to Variant C

- Hero leads with the #1 pain inversion: qualified affiliates, one database, minutes not months.
- Slide 1 attacks manual prospecting/luck; Slide 2 attacks the contact gap (info@ dead ends); Slide 3 attacks unvetted/fake affiliates with activity intel.
- Dark act-break keeps the "stop scraping" pain framing (echoed by both AFPitch and calls).
- FAQ preempts the two live objections: "how are they qualified?" and "can I try it first?" (7-day trial).
