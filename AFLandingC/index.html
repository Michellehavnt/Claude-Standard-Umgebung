FAQs

Questions,
answered.

How is this different from an influencer database? +
Databases are static lists that everyone else already bought. AffiliateFinder actively scans YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Google and the open web for creators currently making content about products like yours. That's proven interest in your category – not a cold row in a CSV.
Where do the email addresses come from? Are they real? +
We enrich every discovered affiliate through multiple contact-data sources and verify the address before you send. Around 90% of affiliates arrive with a verified email, and if one ever bounces, the sequence stops automatically.
Do the AI emails sound like AI wrote them? +
No – each email references the creator's actual recent video or post and follows your saved rules and tone, which the system learns from how you really reply. You can edit every draft before it sends. Most people stop editing after the first week.
Do I stay in control of what gets sent? +
Yes. The AI drafts, you approve. Every email is editable before it goes out, campaigns only start when you launch them, and follow-ups stop the moment someone replies. You set the rules – what to always mention, what never to say – and the AI sticks to them.
What does it cost? +
Plans start at $69/month billed annually ($99 monthly). Every plan begins with a 7-day risk-free trial – see your own affiliate list first, and cancel within the week if it's not for you.
Will this actually get approved by my boss? +
That's what the dashboard is for. Every campaign tracks opens, clicks, replies and signed affiliates, so you can show exactly what was found, who was recruited and what that pipeline is worth – real numbers, ready for the next budget conversation.
