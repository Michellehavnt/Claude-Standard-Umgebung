How does AffiliateFinder find affiliates? +
Our AI scans YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, SEO blogs, LinkedIn and newsletters for creators and publishers already promoting products in your niche, then ranks them by audience fit, engagement and conversion signals.
Do I get contact details? +
Yes — every qualified match is enriched with verified contact data wherever available, so you can start outreach immediately.
Which niches does it work for? +
Any niche where affiliates and creators are active — e-commerce, SaaS, info products, finance, health, and more. Describe your product and see your matches.
How is this different from an affiliate network? +
Networks show you who signed up to the network. AffiliateFinder searches the open web for who is actually selling in your category right now — including affiliates promoting your competitors.
